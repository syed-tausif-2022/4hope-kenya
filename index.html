Our Mission

Built from Nothing.
For Everyone.

4Hope Kenya was founded after watching a previous program collapse due to mismanagement — while orphanage students in Nairobi kept showing up every week asking when the bikes would come. We could not walk away.

Every lesson is engineered to run on a single phone — no computers, no laptops, no infrastructure required. That design choice is what lets us deliver a real engineering curriculum to students who would otherwise have zero access to one.

"These aren't statistics. These are real kids with real dreams. They just need the right people in their corner." — Syed Rahman, Founder

The Bike Build

Three bikes engineered for Kenyan terrain — rough roads, a $161 budget, repairable with basic tools.

⛰️
Mountain Bike
Aluminum Alloy 6061. 29" wheels. Mechanical disc brakes. Built for unpaved roads and heavy loads.
✅ CAD Complete
🛣️
Touring Bike
50kg cargo capacity. Designed for delivery and water collection routes across Nairobi.
🔄 In Progress
⚡
E-Bike
Arduino motor control, regenerative braking. Solar powered for communities with no electricity.

Who We Serve

23 Students.
Two Orphanages.
One Mission.

Children's Garden Home in Nairobi has cared for over 2,000 children since 2001 — providing shelter, meals and community to nearly 250 children today. Engineering education and access to computers is the one thing they haven't had — until now.

Less than 10% of Kenyans own transportation. A bike is survival infrastructure — water collection, school access, safety at night.

Student Story
"I fixed the weld myself. Like you taught me."

One of our students called at 2AM from Kenya. After learning to fix it himself, he called back three months later to say he had just taught a younger kid the same repair. That is why 4Hope exists.
