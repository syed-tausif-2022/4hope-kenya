🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of what we collect and how we protect it. We're a small student-led nonprofit, not a corporation — so this is written to be clear, not legalistic.

Last updated: August 2026

What We Collect

This site uses Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use it — pages viewed, general location (city/country level), device type and how long people stay. This data is anonymized and we don't use it to identify individual visitors.

If you email us or submit a contact form on this site, we receive whatever you send us — typically your email address, name if you provide one, and your message. We use this only to respond to you and never sell or share it with third parties.

Photos & Stories of the Students We Work With

4Hope Kenya works directly with children at partner centers in Nairobi, and we take that responsibility seriously. Our policy is simple:

  • We only publish a child's name, photo or personal story when we have documented consent from the child's guardian or our partner organization.
  • Where that consent hasn't been confirmed, we describe our work in general terms — no names, no institution-identifying details paired with personal stories.
  • If you ever believe we've published something about a specific child without proper consent, tell us and we will review and address it right away.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to distinguish you from other visitors. You can disable this at any time through your browser's privacy settings or a browser extension that blocks analytics scripts — the site works fully either way.

Questions About Your Privacy

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, reach out any time.
